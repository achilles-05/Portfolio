## Projects

### 01 · Vortex-Induced Vibrations in Wind Turbine Blades
**`CFD` `Aeroelastics` `Machine Learning` `ANSYS Fluent`**

End-to-end aeroelastic–CFD–ML framework for analysing vortex-induced vibrations in
idling 15 MW offshore wind turbine blades. Integrated high-fidelity CFD with a Gaussian
Process Regressor achieving ~0.8% RMSE stress prediction, and derived a pitch control
strategy reducing peak flap-wise stresses by ~20%.

> 📄 **Accepted at ICSV32 — 32nd International Congress on Sound and Vibration,
> Istanbul, Turkey, July 2026**
