## About Me

I am a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at Monash University, Melbourne,
specialising in computational fluid dynamics, fluid-structure interaction, and autonomous
systems. My work spans wind energy aeroelastics, UAV flight control, ML-integrated
simulation pipelines, and robotic systems design.

I have experience at MMAPT Robotics, Bosch Australia (robotic automation), MEIL (power
plant projects), WindStream Technologies (CFD for helical VAWTs), Feynman Aerospace
and Skyline Space. My research on vortex-induced vibrations in

Congress on Sound and Vibration (ICSV32), Istanbul, July 2026 - placing my work
among fewer than 2% of Indian submissions accepted to this conference.

My long-term focus is advanced aerodynamics and energy systems engineering, with the
goal of contributing to next-generation UAV and wind energy technology at a global level.

## Projects

### 01 · Vortex-Induced Vibrations in Wind Turbine Blades
**`CFD` `Aeroelastics` `Machine Learning` `ANSYS Fluent`**

Aeroelastic CFD (dynamic meshing, k-ω SST) with a Gaussian Process model reconstructing
blade stress in 15 MW offshore wind turbine blades from sparse sensor data at ~0.8% RMSE.
Derived a pitch control strategy reducing peak flap-wise stresses by ~20%.

> 📄 **Published Mar. 2026 — S. Kumawat, N. Datta, International Congress on Sound and
> Vibration (ICSV32), Istanbul, Turkey. Peer-reviewed, accepted on the first attempt with
> unanimous reviewer scores of 10/10**
